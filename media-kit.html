Voice & naming

The name

Esbee is a bee. It survives being spoken, which an acronym does not, and it reads as a name rather than a claim — no Protocol, Labs, Finance or Capital doing the work.

What makes it worth keeping is what a bee already is — the thing the contract is:

A hive
is a membership that acts as one — what a DAO claims to be, and what quadratic voting is trying to make true.
Workers and store
put in and hold: the pool's members deposit, the treasury holds.
Honey
is yield that accrues slowly and is drawn down later, which is a six-month bond almost exactly.
A honeycomb cell
is a ready-made mark: geometric, legible at 16px, and unlike most crypto logos it is not a coin, a shield, or a chain.

Writing it

Esbee, capital E, never ESBEE or esBee. Esbee DAO for the governance contract; the pool or bond-staker for the ledger it operates. Write it as a word — not as initials, and not with the letters spaced or separated.

One caveat worth stating plainly: a search turned up no crypto or DAO project using the name, which is a first signal and not a clearance. A real trademark and handle check is still owed before anything is printed on anything.

The mark & social assets

A honeycomb cell, with a hint of neighbouring cells above and below, and three tapering bands beneath that read as a bee once noticed and as nothing at all until then.

The strokes sit on the cell's own diagonals, so at small sizes the mark reads as structure and only resolves into a bee up close. It carries its own palette and flips with prefers-color-scheme, so it can sit directly on a page without a plate behind it.
